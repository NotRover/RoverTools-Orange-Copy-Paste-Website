Orange Copy Paste captures the text, images and files you copy, keeps a searchable history alongside your notes, and syncs them across your devices. Sync is end-to-end encrypted: items are encrypted on your computer, and the server cannot read what you copied. Cloud sync is optional. The app is fully usable offline.

## The pieces

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Clipboard history](/docs/clipboard-history/) | Every copy lands in a history with no entry limit that you can search, filter, pin, save, and tag with groups. |
| [Quick paste](/docs/quick-paste/) | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> opens a popup at your cursor. Press a number and that entry is pasted. |
| [Notes](/docs/notes/) | A full editor with task lists, tables, code blocks and attachments, living next to your clips. |
| [Cloud sync](/docs/cloud-sync/) | Optional, end-to-end encrypted sync of history and notes across devices, in realtime, passive, or manual mode. |
| [Spaces](/docs/spaces/) | Live shared feeds between people. Share a clip or note into a space and every member sees it in seconds. |

## Principles the app is built on

- **Local first.** No account is required, and everything works with the network gone. Your data lives on your machine.
- **The server cannot read what you copy.** Encryption happens on your computer, and your password never leaves it. The [security model](/docs/security/) lists what the server can and cannot see.
- **Sharing is opt-in.** Nothing is shared unless you shared it, or you switched on automatic sharing for a space. It is off by default.
