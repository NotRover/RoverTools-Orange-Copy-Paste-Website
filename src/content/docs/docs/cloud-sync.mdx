## How to set up sync

1. Open the **Account & Sync** screen from the sidebar.
2. Create an account:
   - With email: on the **Sign up** tab, enter your email and a password of at least 8 characters, then click **Create account**.
   - With Google: click **Continue with Google** and finish in your browser. Back in the app, you still set a password under **Set your password**. That password is your encryption secret, and it never leaves your device.
3. Save your **recovery code** when the app shows it: **Copy** it or **Save as file**, keep it somewhere safe, and click **I saved my recovery code**. It is shown once and stored nowhere.

When it worked, the screen shows your account, with this computer listed under **Devices** as "this device". Sign in with the same email and password on your other computers to sync them too.

Your password does double duty: it logs you in *and* it decrypts your data, but the two jobs use two different keys derived from it on your device. Sign-in sends only the login key. The key that decrypts your data never leaves your machine, so nothing on the server can stand in for it. The full picture is in the [security model](/docs/security/).

## Sync modes

The mode is per device, so a laptop can sync live while a desktop stays manual. New devices start in **Realtime**.

</DocMock>

| Mode | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Realtime** | Items from your other devices arrive the moment they are copied, and what you copy here uploads right away. |
| **Passive** | Items from your other devices arrive every 5 minutes, or when you press Refresh. What you copy here still uploads right away. |
| **Manual** | Nothing new uploads on its own. Pick what to send with *Upload to cloud*, on an item or on a selection. Items already in the cloud stay up to date, removing one still removes it, and Refresh brings down what your other devices sent. |

Spaces are not affected by the mode: what you share to a space goes out right away, and what others share with you always arrives live.

## What syncs

Clipboard entries, notes, and some of your preferences. Which settings travel and which stay on each computer is listed in [Which settings sync](/docs/settings/#which-settings-sync).

## Files, images and folders

Images and files ride along, up to **5 MB per entry**. For an entry holding several files, or a folder, the 5 MB covers all of them together. A folder syncs as a single entry and unpacks on the other device. Anything over the cap is skipped and the app tells you; nothing is dropped silently.

Accounts start with **50 MB** of encrypted file storage. The storage bar turns amber at 85%, and the app notifies you at 90%. Only images and files you upload count against it; text and notes take no file storage.

Two more ceilings keep an account bounded, and the app leaves anything past them on the device rather than failing:

- A single entry's text is capped at about **384 KB**. A larger clip stays local.
- Up to **3,000 items** sync per account. Beyond that, new items stay local until you make room.

The Account screen shows your storage, a separate synced-item count, per-type breakdowns, and a cloud view of everything stored. When something will not fit, it lands in a *not synced* list grouped by reason, with **Try again** once you have space.

## Devices

Each installation registers as a device, named automatically from its platform. The Account screen lists them with live online/offline presence, and you can revoke a device you no longer use. You cannot revoke the device you are signed in on; sign out on that one instead. A revoked device has to sign in again before it syncs.

To stop this device sending anything new, switch it to **Manual**. Items already in the cloud still stay up to date, and spaces keep syncing; to stop completely, sign out.

To change your password, click **Change password** on a signed-in device, enter your **Current password** and the new one, and click **Save password**. Your synced items stay readable: the key that decrypts them stays the same, and only what wraps it changes.

## Deleting and removing

Deleting a synced entry asks you to confirm first, because the removal travels to your other devices as well. The prompt spells out what the delete affects, and a *Don't ask again* option turns it off:

- **Your own items** leave all your synced devices.
- **An item someone shared with you** leaves all your devices, but stays in the space for everyone else.
- **An item you shared into a space** also leaves that space for everyone.

*Remove from cloud* is different: it takes an item, or a whole selection, off the server and your other devices, but keeps the copy on the device you are on. Use it to free up cloud space without losing your local history.

## Offline and conflicts

Offline changes queue locally and drain in order when you reconnect. Conflicts resolve to the most recent edit. A delete you make offline is queued like any other change and reaches your other devices once you are back online.

## How to reset a forgotten password

1. On the **Sign in** tab, click **Forgot?**, enter your email, and click **Send reset link**. Ask from the computer you will reset on: the link only works in the app that asked for it.
2. Open the link in the email. The app opens **Set a new password**.
3. Enter the new password twice and click **Save password**.

On a computer that has been signed in to this account before, that is all: your synced data stays readable. On one that has not, the app also asks for your **Recovery code** and you click **Use the recovery code**. Signing in on a computer you have used before works too.

If you have no recovery code and no computer that was signed in, **Start over with a new key** gets you back into the account, but everything synced under the old key can no longer be read, on any device, by anyone, including us.
