Everything you copy lands in a searchable history with no entry limit, and by default it stays when the app or your PC restarts. This page covers what gets captured, the limits, pinning and saving, groups, search and filters, and acting on many entries at once.

## What gets captured

A background watcher checks the clipboard a few times a second and records every new copy: plain text, links, rich HTML (with a plain-text fallback kept alongside), images, files and folders. Copies made by the app itself are never re-captured, and a copy identical to the newest entry is skipped, so the history stays duplicate-free.

Images are saved to disk at full quality the moment they arrive and previewed from there, which keeps the app light even with large screenshots. Copied videos play directly on the card with play, seek and mute controls. Multi-file copies show a thumbnail strip with a +N chip that expands to the full list.

The entry currently held by the operating system clipboard is marked with an accent border and an *In clipboard* chip.

## Limits

- History has **no entry limit**. Nothing is dropped as it grows.
- With **Keep history across restarts** off, only pinned and Saved entries, and anything in the cloud or a space, stay when the app restarts. It is on by default. See [Settings](/docs/settings/#history).
- Up to **10 entries can be pinned**. The 11th pin shows a toast instead.
- A single copy larger than about **4 MB** of text, rich text, or file paths is refused with a toast rather than truncated, and your ordinary paste still works. Images are exempt, since they are stored as files on disk. This is separate from the [cloud sync](/docs/cloud-sync/) size caps.

## Pin vs Save

These are two different promises:

- **Pin** puts an entry in the quick-paste popup's Pinned tab. Maximum 10.
- **Save** keeps an entry through **Clear history**, and through restarts even with **Keep history across restarts** off. It is independent of pinning.

**Clear history** keeps everything pinned or saved. **Auto-save copied entries** is on by default and adds every new capture to Saved, so while it is on, **Clear history** removes only entries captured while it was off. To have **Clear history** remove new entries, turn off **Auto-save copied entries** in [Settings](/docs/settings/#history). Entries it already saved stay Saved.

## Groups

<DocMock scale={1.25}>
	<GroupManager />
</DocMock>

Groups are colored tags you define. Create, rename, recolor and delete them from the Group Manager, or assign them from an entry's right-click menu. A group keeps the same color everywhere (you can override it with the color swatches), and deleting a group gives you a 5 second undo toast.

`Pinned` and `Saved` are system groups; those names are reserved.

## Search and filters

<DocMock scale={0.8}>
	<FilterCard />
</DocMock>

Search matches entry content and group names. Filters stack on top:

- **Quick:** Pinned, Saved, and, once you are signed in, Mine or From others.
- **Type:** text, url, html, image, video, document, file, folder.
- **Cloud:** once you are signed in, Any, In cloud, or Local. Once you are in a space, the same section also filters by sharing: Any, In a space, Not shared, or one chip per space.
- **Groups:** any of your groups.
- **Date:** Any time, Today, 7 days, or Range, which lets you pick the start and end dates.

Every filter option shows the number of items it would leave you *before* you apply it.

While you are signed out, or while the app is reconnecting to your account, the Cloud section and Mine or From others are hidden and do not narrow the list. What you picked in them is kept, and applies again when you sign in.

## Bulk actions

<DocMock scale={0.9}>
	<BulkBar flyout={false} />
</DocMock>

Select multiple entries (with range select) to copy them as one item, pin, save, assign groups, share with a space, upload to or remove from your account, remove from a space, or delete, all in one action. Copy joins the selected entries into a single clipboard payload; it is unavailable when the selection mixes text and files, which the clipboard cannot carry as one item. <kbd>Esc</kbd> exits selection mode.

## Opening an entry

A single click copies the entry straight back to the clipboard; a double click opens it in a full viewer. You can swap those with *Click a card to view it* in [Settings](/docs/settings/), so a single click opens and a double click copies. The viewer shows the entry at full size, and images can zoom to their original resolution.

## Sorting and layout

Sort by Newest, Oldest, A to Z, Z to A, or Type. Switch between **Tiles view**, **Grid view** and **Single column** from the top bar. Entries group under collapsible day headers (Today, Yesterday, then dates), and long histories render progressively so scrolling stays smooth.
