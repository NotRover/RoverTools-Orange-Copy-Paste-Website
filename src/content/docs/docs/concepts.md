The app uses a small set of words on its screens and in these docs. This page says what each one means and links to the page where you use it.

## Your clipboard

**Entry.** One thing you copied: text, a link, an image, a file or folder, or rich HTML. Each copy becomes one entry in your history.

**History.** Your recent entries, newest first. It keeps the last **100** entries that are not pinned or saved; when a new one arrives, the oldest unpinned, unsaved one drops off. By default history is cleared when the app restarts. See [Clipboard history](/docs/clipboard-history/).

**Pinned.** An entry you pinned stays at hand on the **Pinned** tab of the paste popup, with its own number. You can pin up to **10** entries. Pinned entries are never dropped from history, survive restarts, and are kept by **Clear all**.

**Saved.** Also a way to keep an entry, but without the 10-entry limit and without a place in the paste popup. Saving puts the entry in the built-in **Saved** group. Saved entries are kept exactly like pinned ones. Use pins for the few things you paste all the time, and Saved for everything else worth keeping.

**Group.** A label you put on entries and notes, with a color, to filter them later. One entry can be in several groups. Groups are shared between clipboard entries and notes.

**Note.** A document you write in the app, next to your history. Notes are always kept, and can hold clipboard entries inside them. See [Notes](/docs/notes/).

**Paste popup and capture popup.** The two small windows that open at your cursor from any app: <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> to paste an entry by number, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd> to capture the selection and see what was grabbed. The numbers in the paste popup are called **slots**. See [Quick paste and capture](/docs/quick-paste/).

## Your account and devices

**Account.** Optional. You only need one for sync and spaces. Its password does two jobs: it signs you in, and it decrypts your synced data. See [Cloud sync](/docs/cloud-sync/).

**Device.** Each computer where you sign in to your account. Devices are listed on the **Account & Sync** screen, where you can see which are online and revoke one you no longer use.

**Sync mode.** How this device exchanges items with your other devices: **Realtime**, **Passive** or **Manual**. Each device has its own. See [Sync modes](/docs/cloud-sync/#sync-modes).

**Recovery code.** A code the app shows once when you set up your account (also called a backup code). With it, you can reset a forgotten password on a new computer and keep your synced data. It is stored nowhere, so keep it safe. See [How to reset a forgotten password](/docs/cloud-sync/#how-to-reset-a-forgotten-password).

**Master key.** The key that decrypts everything you sync. It is created on your computer and never leaves it unencrypted. You never see or type it: your password, a computer you have signed in on before, or your recovery code decrypts it. See the [security model](/docs/security/).

## Sharing

**Space.** A shared feed that any number of people can join (sometimes called a shared clipboard or a team clipboard). Items shared into it reach every member in seconds. You can be in several spaces, and one item can be in several spaces. See [Spaces and sharing](/docs/spaces/).

**Owner and member.** The person who created a space is its **owner**. Everyone else is a **member**. Only the owner can invite by email, remove members, or delete the space.

**Invite code, and request to join.** Each space has an 8-character code, which also comes as a link. Someone who uses it sends a **request to join**, which the owner, or any member if the owner allows it, has to approve. An email invite from the owner skips the request.

**Space key.** Each space has its own key, which is what lets members read its items. The app hands it to new members for you. When a member is removed, the space gets a new key.

**Mine and From others.** Filters in your history. **Mine** is what you copied yourself, on any of your devices; **From others** is what other people shared into your spaces.
