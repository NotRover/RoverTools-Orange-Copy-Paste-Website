Two popups work from any app: one pastes a recent or pinned entry by number, and the other captures what you have selected and shows what it grabbed.

## The paste popup

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> anywhere. A small popup opens at your cursor with two tabs:

- **Recent:** your recent clipboard entries, newest first, as a scrollable searchable list.
- **Pinned:** up to 10 pinned entries.

	<QuickPastePopup />
</DocMock>

Each row has a slot number. Press <kbd>1</kbd> through <kbd>9</kbd> (then <kbd>0</kbd>) and that entry is pasted into the app you came from. You can also click a row, or pick one with the arrow keys and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. Hovering a row shows **Pin**, **Copy without pasting** and **Delete**. Every key the popup takes, including paste-and-remove, is in the [shortcuts reference](/docs/shortcuts/#inside-the-paste-popup).

The number of slots shown is configurable from 3 to 10 (default 3) in [Settings](/docs/settings/). If an entry points at files that no longer exist on disk, the app tells you instead of pasting a broken path.

## The capture popup

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd> with something selected in any app. The selection is captured to history and a small receipt appears at your cursor showing what was grabbed: the type, a preview, and buttons to **Pin**, add to **Groups**, **Share**, or **Delete** it right there. To save it, press <kbd>S</kbd> or pick **Saved** in the groups list.

<DocMock>
	<CapturePopup />
</DocMock>

It closes on <kbd>Esc</kbd>, when you click elsewhere, or with the close button. Its keys are in the [shortcuts reference](/docs/shortcuts/#inside-the-capture-popup).

Plain <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>C</kbd> still captures to history too, just without the receipt.

## Behavior details

- Both popups are toggles: the same hotkey closes them.
- **Copy without pasting** leaves the paste popup open. Turn off *Keep paste popup open after copying* in [Settings](/docs/settings/) to close it instead.
- Drag either popup by its header to move it where you want.
- Popups stay on top, skip the taskbar, and are clamped to your monitor's work area, so they never open half off-screen.
- A brief *Copied* / *Pasted* toast appears at the bottom right if you have the in-app popup notifications enabled.

Full key list: [Shortcuts reference](/docs/shortcuts/).
