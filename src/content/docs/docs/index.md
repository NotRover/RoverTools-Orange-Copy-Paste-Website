---
title: What is Orange Copy Paste?
description: A smart clipboard for Windows and Linux with history, notes, quick paste, and end-to-end encrypted sync.
---

Orange Copy Paste captures the text, images and files you copy, keeps a searchable history alongside your notes, and syncs them across your devices. Sync is end-to-end encrypted: every key stays on your machines and the server only ever stores ciphertext. Cloud sync is optional. The app is fully usable offline.

![The Orange Copy Paste window showing clipboard history, with an entry selected and the sidebar of screens down the left.](/screenshots/app-window.png)

## The pieces

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Clipboard history](/docs/clipboard-history/) | Every copy lands in a rolling history of 100 entries you can search, filter, pin, save, and tag with groups. |
| [Quick paste](/docs/quick-paste/) | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> opens a popup at your cursor. Press a number and that entry is pasted. |
| [Notes](/docs/notes/) | A full editor with task lists, tables, code blocks and attachments, living next to your clips. |
| [Cloud sync](/docs/cloud-sync/) | Optional, end-to-end encrypted sync of history and notes across devices, in realtime, passive, or manual mode. |
| [Spaces](/docs/spaces/) | Live shared feeds between people. Share a clip or note into a space and every member sees it in seconds. |

## Where to start

1. [Install the app](/docs/getting-started/) on Windows or Linux.
2. Copy a few things, then press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd>.
3. When you want your history on another machine, [set up sync](/docs/cloud-sync/).

## Principles the app is built on

- **Local first.** No account is required, and everything works with the network gone. Your data lives on your machine.
- **The server can never read your data.** Encryption happens on your device, and your password never leaves it. See the [security model](/docs/security/).
- **Sharing is opt-in.** Nothing is shared unless you shared it, or you switched on a filter that says so. Send filters are off by default.
- **Quiet.** The app lives in the tray, and the noisiest options ship disabled.
