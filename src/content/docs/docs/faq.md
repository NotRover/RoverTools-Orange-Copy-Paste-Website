## Do I need an account?

No. Everything except sync and spaces works without one, and you can add an account later without losing your history. See [Cloud sync](/docs/cloud-sync/).

## Is it free?

Yes. A cloud account includes 50 MB of encrypted storage for images and files; text and notes do not count against it. See [Files, images and folders](/docs/cloud-sync/#files-images-and-folders).

## Can the server, or anyone at RoverTools, read my clips?

No. Items are encrypted on your computer, and the server holds no key that opens them. The [security model](/docs/security/#what-the-server-can-see) lists what the server can and cannot see.

## What if I forget my password?

Reset it from the sign-in screen. On a computer you have signed in on before, your data stays readable; elsewhere you also need your recovery code. See [How to reset a forgotten password](/docs/cloud-sync/#how-to-reset-a-forgotten-password).

## Why did my history disappear after a restart?

Unpinned history is cleared on restart unless you turn on **Keep history across app restarts** in [Settings](/docs/settings/). Pinned and saved entries always stay.

## Why was a file not synced?

Synced files are capped at 5 MB per entry. A bigger one stays on that computer, and the app lists it as not synced instead of dropping it. See [Files, images and folders](/docs/cloud-sync/#files-images-and-folders).

## Does someone I share with see my whole clipboard?

No. You share items one at a time into a space. Automatic sharing is off until you turn it on, and never shares older items. See [Spaces and sharing](/docs/spaces/#sharing-into-a-space).

## Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. A Mac build for Apple silicon and Intel is planned. Today the app runs on Windows and [Linux](/docs/linux/).

## Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

In the [app repository's issues](https://github.com/NotRover/RoverTools-Orange-Copy-Paste-App/issues).
